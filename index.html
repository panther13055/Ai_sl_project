Inside the agent's reasoning

Top affordable plans

Higher utility is better. Scores are comparative, not percentages.

View the scoring model

Utility = −8 × discomfort − energy weight × kW − 0.5 × cooling switches.

Discomfort combines squared temperature error and a 6-point penalty for an occupied, dim room without light. Energy weights: Comfort 0.5, Balanced 4, Eco 15. Plans above the spending limit are rejected.

THE AI MODEL

Sense. Compare. Act. Repeat.

01 / Sense

Read occupancy, temperature, daylight and user preferences.

02 / Compare

Score available appliance plans and reject plans above the limit.

03 / Act

Apply the highest-scoring feasible combination for five simulated minutes.

04 / Repeat

Update temperature and energy, then evaluate the next decision.

Educational prototype with fixed rules and a utility function. No trained ML model, real sensors, actual device control or measured savings. All power values and thermal dynamics are illustrative.
