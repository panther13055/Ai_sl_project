Scoring formula

Utility = −8 × discomfort − energy weight × kW − 0.5 × cooling switches.

Discomfort combines squared temperature error and a 6-point penalty for an occupied, dim room without light. Energy weights: Comfort 0.5, Balanced 4, Eco 15. Plans above the spending limit are rejected.

How the agent works
  1. Read occupancy, temperature, daylight and your settings.
  2. Compare appliance combinations using predicted comfort and power use.
  3. Select the highest-scoring plan within the spending limit.
  4. Apply it for 5 simulated minutes, update the readings and repeat.

This is a utility-based AI simulation with fixed rules. Sensors, appliance power and temperature changes are illustrative. It does not control real devices or use a trained machine-learning model.
